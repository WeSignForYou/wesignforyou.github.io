Apostille Services

An apostille is a certificate that authenticates the signature of a public official on your document — a County Clerk, County Recorder, Notary Public or the State Registrar, for example — so that it can be used in another country.

  • Vital records (like birth, death & marriage certificates)
  • Educational records (like diplomas & transcripts)
  • Court, corporate & business documents
  • Notarized documents & sworn statements
  • and many more
